Wiring a contract to cykablyat.vip

The borrow screen is built and live as the first page of the site. It has no wallet code in it — the page cannot reach a blockchain at all yet. This is what it needs in order to, what it already assumes, and how it gets switched on.

1. What the page needs

Four things. Nothing else is required to arm it.

  1. Contract addressThe deployed lending contract.
  2. ABIThe compiled interface, as JSON.
  3. Chain IDPinned on every write, so a wallet on the wrong network cannot send.
  4. RPC originsThe exact hostnames the page may talk to. See section 4 — this one is not a formality.

2. What the page already assumes

These are compiled into it. If the deployment disagrees with any of them, the page is wrong and it needs to be corrected before it is switched on — not after.

AssumptionValueWhere
MAX_LTV80% — the most you may borrow tosrc/lib/health.ts
LIQ_LTV90% — the point of liquidationsrc/lib/health.ts
LiquidationAll of it. No partial, no grace.stated on the page
Ruler scale0 → 100, alwaysrulerPct
UI cap79%, not 80 — see belowUI_MAX_LTV

Two of those deserve a sentence each

The ruler is drawn 0 → 100 everywhere. The 80 and 90 marks therefore land in the same place on the borrow form and on every position, which is the whole reason several loans can be compared at a glance. Scaling to the liquidation threshold instead — ltv / 90 * 100 — puts 80% at 89% of the bar and silently destroys that.

The page stops at 79%, not 80%. The contract rounds three ways in its own favour: collateral value down, debt value up, and the final ratio up. A loan computed at exactly 80% therefore arrives on-chain as 8001 basis points and is refused. Everything offered as a maximum is shaded below the limit first, so "the most you can borrow" is a number that actually goes through.

3. Two behaviours that must not be changed

Both are already correct in the page. They are written down because both are easy to "tidy up" into something wrong.

Adding collateral and repaying are never blocked

The 80% cap applies to borrowing and to withdrawing collateral. It does not apply to adding collateral or to repaying — those two only ever improve a position, so they are allowed at any ratio, including when the position is already past 80%. Gating all four together looks tidy and breaks the one action that saves someone who is about to be liquidated.

One risk scale, not three

Health comes from healthOf() and nothing else: under 80 healthy, 80–90 at risk, 90 and over liquidatable. Adding a second rule-of-thumb alongside it produces rows that are red next to a button that says everything is fine. The words are rendered too, not just the colour, so the state does not depend on being able to see red.

4. Switching it on

Three edits, all in this repository.

FileChange
src/components/ProtocolSection.tsxLIVE from false to true
public/_headersconnect-src gains the RPC origins
src/app/layout.tsxthe same change, in the meta tag

not this connect-src 'self' * — or default-src widened, or a wildcard host.

this connect-src 'self' https://rpc.example https://rpc2.example — named origins only.

That single line is what currently makes "a script on this page cannot ask for your wallet" a fact rather than a hope. Widening it to everything removes the protection entirely while looking like a one-word change.
